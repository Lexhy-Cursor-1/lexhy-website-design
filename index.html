Why a member firm is allowed to rely on it

Singapore law practices may rely on a third party's client due diligence where three conditions hold. The network is built so that all three do, on every matter, by default.

  1. The verifier is supervised. Bethel Chambers is a Singapore law practice subject to the Legal Profession (Prevention of Money Laundering, Financing of Terrorism and Proliferation Financing) Rules, and supervised for compliance with them.
  2. Its measures are documented. The procedure, the evidence and the sign-off are recorded on the platform, so a relying firm can see what was done before it relies.
  3. Source documents are produced on request. Immediately, through the platform, with your release — which is the condition most reliance arrangements fail in practice.

Reliance is not delegation. Each member firm remains responsible for its own due diligence and for its own reporting obligations. What the passport removes is the repetition, not the duty — and any firm can always ask you for more.

Curation

The bar is an admission condition for the practice, retested every year.

A network is only worth joining if the next firm admitted is as good as the last. Four gates, applied to the practice rather than to the individual you happen to meet, and retested every year. The fourth one is new, and it is the one most firms now fail.

Gate 1 Trained at an Am Law 100 or Magic Circle firm 70% of a member firm's fee earners must clear this. Below that line the practice is not admitted, however well regarded it is locally. 70%
Gate 2 Five years post-qualification, minimum Nobody reaches a client here while they are still learning the job. The median across the network is eleven years. 5 yrs

Admitted One firm per market Admitted for the specialism the network lacks, and holding the seat on referral volume rather than tenure. Six markets, six firms. 1 of 1

Why five years, not three

Agents have collapsed the work that used to teach junior lawyers judgment. Someone who qualified into a practice where the first draft already exists needs longer before they are the one deciding whether it is right.

Why fluency is a gate, not a bonus

A lawyer who cannot direct an agent charges you for work the agent would have done in minutes. One who trusts it blindly charges you for a mistake. Both fail the same test.

Retested every year

Admission is not a certificate. The ratio, the rankings and the override rates are re-measured annually, and a firm that drifts below the line is told before a client is.
