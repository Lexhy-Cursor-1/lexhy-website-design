# Rebuild Lexhy as a management-company site

## Scope
- Replace the current marketing experience with six restrained editorial pages: Home, Private wealth, How it works, Member firms, Contact, and Invitation.
- Remove all legacy public routes and prevent old product, pricing, review, blog, chatbot, and AI-first content from appearing.
- Draw a new solid two-hook SVG mark based on the supplied official silhouette, with no internal letters or pattern, and use it in the header and favicon.

## Experience
- Apply the supplied stone, paper, charcoal, muted, and hairline palette throughout.
- Use Newsreader for editorial headings and Libre Franklin for navigation, body copy, and forms.
- Build a thin sticky header, generous typographic sections separated by lines, restrained rectangular controls, and a compact legal footer.
- Keep mobile navigation minimal: brand and Contact remain visible while intermediate links collapse.

## Content and interactions
- Use the supplied copy and disclosures verbatim where specified.
- Add accessible FAQ accordions on Home and How it works, plus FAQ content on Contact.
- Build validated Contact and Invitation forms with required acknowledgement checkboxes and exact success messages; submissions remain local because no delivery service was specified.
- Ensure old URLs resolve to the not-found page rather than redirecting into the new journeys.

## Search and page identity
- Add accurate global metadata and Organization structured data without classifying Lexhy as a law firm or legal service.
- Set unique client-side titles, descriptions, canonical URLs, and page-specific FAQ structured data for all six routes.
- Add a sitemap and crawler rules for the six public pages.

## Verification
- Confirm the preview builds without errors.
- Test all six routes, navigation, form validation and success states, FAQ interactions, favicon, desktop layout, and mobile header in the browser.
